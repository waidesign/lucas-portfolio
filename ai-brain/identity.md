## Brand Voice

When writing copy for this portfolio, follow these principles:

- **Confident but not arrogant** — State expertise plainly, let the work speak
- **Clear and simple** — Avoid jargon; favour plain English that any stakeholder can understand
- **Concise** — Short sentences, active voice, no filler
- **Internationally readable** — The audience is global; avoid idioms or region-specific phrasing
- **Action-oriented CTAs** — e.g. "Explore case studies", "Schedule a chat", "Get Resume" — direct, no fluff

## Design Philosophy

- Detail-obsessed: micro-animations, precise spacing, consistent tokens
- User-first: every decision justified by clarity and usability
- Premium feel: dark modes, subtle gradients, grayscale-to-color reveals
- Mobile-first implementation, desktop-polished presentation
